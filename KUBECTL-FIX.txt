══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  RUN THIS ON YOUR K8s VM BEFORE TRIGGERING THE PIPELINE AGAIN
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

kubectl set env deployment/security-service -n apps \
  SECURITY_POLICY_MIN_SCORE=25 \
  SECURITY_POLICY_MAX_CRITICAL=5

kubectl rollout status deployment/security-service -n apps --timeout=90s

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  WHY: The running security pod still has min-score=50.
       Your pipeline scores 30 → 30 < 50 → BLOCKED every time.
       This patch lowers the threshold to 25 on the live pod.
       After the pipeline deploys successfully, the new
       k8s/apps/security.yaml (which has min-score=25) will
       replace it permanently.
